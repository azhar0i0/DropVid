    On your phone

    Where to find the video afterwards

    Phones put downloads in a folder, not your gallery. Here's how to move a video to your camera roll.

    iPhone and iPad

    Safari, iOS 13 or later
    1. Tap Download, then confirm with Download in the pop-up.
    2. Open the Files app and go to Downloads.
    3. Open the video, tap Share, then Save Video to add it to Photos.

    Android

    Chrome, Samsung Internet and most browsers
    1. Tap Download. A bar shows the progress at the bottom of the screen.
    2. Tap Open when it finishes, or find it in your Downloads folder.
    3. The video also appears in Gallery or Google Photos under Downloads.

    FAQ

    Questions people ask

    Still stuck? Try fetching the link again. Platforms change often, and most failures fix themselves within a day or two.

    Which links work?

    Public Instagram reels and posts, Facebook videos and reels, TikTok videos, and YouTube videos and Shorts, including short vm.tiktok.com, fb.watch and youtu.be links. Private accounts and posts that need a login won't work.

    Why did my link fail?

    The post may be private, deleted or age-restricted. Instagram also blocks some requests at busy times, so wait a minute and try again.

    Why do some YouTube qualities have no sound?

    YouTube stores the picture and the sound of most videos as two separate files, and joining them takes more processing than Dropvid's servers can do. Qualities marked "No sound" are silent video. Download the Audio option as well if you need the sound. Large YouTube files can also be slow to arrive.

    Is it free?

    Yes. There's no account, no subscription and no daily limit.

    Do you keep the videos I download?

    No. The video streams from the platform to your device and isn't stored on our servers.

    Will the TikTok have a watermark?

    It depends on what TikTok provides for that video. When a version without the watermark is available, it's marked in the quality list.

    Can I download someone else's video?

    Only save videos you made or have permission to use. Reposting someone else's work without permission can break copyright law and the platform's rules.
