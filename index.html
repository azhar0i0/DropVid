Questions

The short answers to what people ask most.

Which links work?
Public Instagram reels and posts, Facebook videos and reels, and TikTok videos, including short vm.tiktok.com links.
Why did a link fail?
The post may be private, deleted, or from an account that requires a login. Instagram also blocks some requests at busy times, so try again in a minute.
Where does the file go?
Your browser's normal downloads folder. On iPhone, open the Files app and look in Downloads.
Do you keep the videos?
No. The file streams through to you and isn't stored, and neither is the link you paste.
Is it free?
Yes, with no account and no download limit.

Only download content you own or have permission to save. Dropvid is not affiliated with Meta or ByteDance.
